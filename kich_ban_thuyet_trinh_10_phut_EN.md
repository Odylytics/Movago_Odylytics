## 1 · Cover (Nghĩa, Quân · 0:20)

**Nghĩa:** Good morning. We are team Odylytics, and this is Movago. Move smarter, go safer. I'm Nghĩa.

**Quân:** And I'm Quân. Movago checks every freight invoice against the shipment and the agreed rates, so forwarders stop overpaying.

## 2 · How Freight Works (Quân · 0:50)

**Quân:** Before the problem, the picture. A shipper, an exporter or importer, wants its goods moved. It doesn't call the shipping line. It hires a freight forwarder.

*[Point to the forwarder card.]* The forwarder quotes, books and organises the whole shipment. It hires the shipping line, the trucking company, the customs broker, the port and an overseas agent, and it pays every one of them. Then it bills the shipper.

*[Point to the dashed arrow.]* Each supplier sends its own invoice or debit note back to the forwarder. Quote, book, move, document, and finally, pay suppliers. That last step is where Movago works.

## 3 · Business Problem (Nghĩa · 0:35)

**Nghĩa:** And that step is getting more expensive. Logistics costs sixteen to seventeen percent of Vietnam's GDP, against about eleven percent worldwide. Container handling charges rose about fifty percent in ten years.

In typical US audits, three to six percent of freight invoices carry billing errors. Vietnam has no published figure yet, and our pilot will measure it. Meanwhile, every charge is still checked by hand, across three files: the invoice, the shipment and the rate card.

## 4 · Target Business & Users (Quân · 0:30)

**Quân:** Our first customer is a mid-sized ocean forwarder in Ho Chi Minh City, one of four to four and a half thousand in Vietnam. The person who checks supplier invoices uses Movago every day. Operations confirms the shipment, pricing confirms the rate, and the chief accountant or CFO, who owns the budget, approves payment. The pilot starts small: one team, one lane, one or two suppliers.

## 5 · Business Insight (Nghĩa · 0:35)

**Nghĩa:** Here is why it's hard: the correct amount depends on the shipment. Under the same fee code, a forty-foot container costs fifty-four to sixty percent more than a twenty-foot one, at both ONE and Maersk.

Carriers also name fees differently, in Vietnamese and in English. Missing proof goes back to email and Zalo. And when a supplier resends an invoice under a new file name, it can be paid twice.

## 6 · Why Now (Quân · 0:25)

**Quân:** Why now? Vietnam processed eight and a half billion e-invoices in 2025, and new e-invoice rules took effect on the first of July this year. Trade passed one trillion dollars in ten months, up thirty-two percent. Invoices now arrive as data. Matching them to shipments and rates is still manual.

## 7 · Proposed Solution (Nghĩa · 0:35)

**Nghĩa:** Movago replaces three windows with one screen. It matches each invoice to its shipment and the approved rate card, recalculates every line, and returns one of three results: Matched, Discrepancy or Missing basis.

Behind every difference, both sources open side by side. Movago never pays anything. People confirm, and approvers pay.

## 8 · How AI Helps (Quân · 0:35)

**Quân:** We use AI where it fits. AI reads quotations, invoices and phone scans, and maps fee names to one code, so "Phí chứng từ" and "Documentation fee" both become DOC.

Then a rules engine picks the rate and does the maths. AI never sets the amount, because finance needs results it can audit. Anything the AI is unsure about goes to a person.

## 9 · How It Works (Nghĩa · 0:30)

**Nghĩa:** Four steps. One: AI reads the carrier's quotation, and pricing approves the rate card, which people used to type into Excel. Two: Movago reads each invoice, and a fingerprint blocks resent copies. Three: it matches the shipment and applies the rate in force. Four: each line is checked and confirmed. PDFs and a shipment file go in; a line-by-line report comes out.

## 10 · Mockup / Demo (Nghĩa, Quân · 1:05)

**Quân:** Nghĩa, show them a real case.

**Nghĩa:** This is invoice INV-095, for a container of school uniforms from Singapore to Cat Lai.

*[Point to panel 1.]* Ocean freight matches the rate card. The documentation fee doesn't: fifty-one dollars on the invoice, twenty approved. Thirty-one dollars over, before tax. The invoice adds up, so a total check would have passed it.

*[Point to panel 2.]* One click opens the evidence: invoice page one on the left, rate card line twelve on the right, with the calculation. The line goes to pricing to confirm and stays flagged until they do. Once it is settled, the payment request goes to accounting as one file.

*[Point to panel 3.]* Next day, the supplier resends the same PDF under a new name. Movago recognises the fingerprint, so it is never entered twice.

**Quân:** The data is simulated, and you can try it at movago dot odylytics dot com.

## 11 · User Validation (Nghĩa · 0:35)

**Nghĩa:** We took this to people who do the job. First, we interviewed Ms. Laura Tran, CEO of TB Logistics JSC, who approves supplier payments. Then we surveyed more than fifty professionals in logistics and finance. Seventy-two percent still check invoices by hand, and eighty-two percent would try Movago.

Our pilot goal is thirty percent less time per invoice.

## 12 · Market Size (Quân · 0:35)

**Quân:** Freight audit is about a nine-hundred-million-dollar market worldwide. In Vietnam we size it bottom-up: four thousand forwarders, and we assume one in ten fits our first profile, so about four hundred firms. At three million dong a month, that is a serviceable market of five hundred sixty-five thousand dollars a year. Our target is thirty customers by year three. The pilot will test every one of these assumptions.

## 13 · Feasibility & Business Value (Nghĩa · 0:35)

**Nghĩa:** Is it worth paying for? Take a forwarder with a thousand invoices a month. Save six minutes on each, and the team gets back one hundred hours a month, about two hundred thirty-five dollars. Movago breaks even at three minutes saved.

We sell it as B2B software, priced per unique invoice. The pilot is small on purpose: thirty to one hundred invoice sets, one lane, data in and out by CSV.

## 14 · Competitive Advantage (Quân · 0:35)

**Quân:** No single feature here is unique, and we won't pretend it is. Excel and email check nothing. G.O.L stores freight data but doesn't check it. CargoWise checks invoices, but only if you run CargoWise. ShipCheck audits rates, but not Vietnamese documents.

Movago combines freight logic and Vietnamese documents, and it works next to the systems a forwarder already runs, from G.O.L to MISA.

## 15 · Team (Nghĩa · 0:20)

**Nghĩa:** Movago is built by five co-founders at Odylytics, a student-founded AI team from the UTS and Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology programme. Between us we cover engineering, business analysis, product, design and AI, and we are supported by partners including Leave a Nest, B71, NUSX, Dow, UTS and HCMUT.

## 16 · Why Our Team (Quân · 0:50)

**Quân:** Why us? We ship AI to real users. More than a thousand people have tested our products, and they have passed a million views on social media.

We build fast: six products share one AI core, Movago reuses it, and Includio became a working MVP in a single hackathon. More than ten partner organisations endorse our work, and we are advised by academics from UTS, Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology and Laval University.

To be clear, those results belong to AquaGuard and our other products, not to Movago. What they show is that we finish what we start.

## 17 · Close (Nghĩa, Quân · 0:30)

**Nghĩa:** We are asking for one forwarder in Ho Chi Minh City to run thirty to one hundred invoice sets with us, so we can measure the time saved and the errors caught on real invoices.

**Quân:** Check before you pay. Move smarter. Go safer. Thank you.
