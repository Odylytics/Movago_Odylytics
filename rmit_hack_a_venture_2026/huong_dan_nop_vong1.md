## Introduction

In Round 1, each team must submit an idea proposal addressing a specific problem within Vietnam's financial system. The proposal should demonstrate the feasibility, innovation, market potential, strategic impact, and scalability of the proposed solution.

Round 1 submission will be assessed on both the quality of the team's analysis and the merit of the proposed idea. Teams must demonstrate not only a compelling solution but also a clear, evidence-based rationale for why it addresses the problem effectively.

Each submission must address the following components:

1. Executive Summary
2. Problem Statement & Market Pain
3. Solution Architecture & FinTech Integration
4. Market Size & Competitive Analysis
5. Strategic Contribution & Expected Impact
6. Implementation Roadmap & Scalability

Figures, diagrams, flowcharts, and tables are encouraged where they improve clarity.

## Proposal Structure

### Executive Summary

Provide a concise overview of the chosen pillar, the identified financial problem, the proposed technological solution, the target market, and the anticipated socio-economic impact.

### Problem Statement & Market Pain

- Define a specific problem within Vietnam's financial system and explain its real-world significance and urgency.
- Identify the root causes of the problem, the groups affected, and the limitations of existing solutions or market practices.
- Support the problem analysis with credible data, evidence, or research relevant to Vietnam.

### Solution Architecture & FinTech Integration

- Explain the proposed solution, its core features, and how it addresses the identified root causes.
- Describe the practical operational workflow, identifying the intended users, and how they interact with the solution.
- Explain the technology stack and the role of FinTech technologies in improving efficiency, reliability, or accessibility.
- Demonstrate how Open Innovation, such as Open APIs, shared data, or collaboration among banks, fintechs, and enterprises enable the solution.
- Must include a high-level system topology or flowchart illustrating the end-to-end process.
- Highlight the solution's innovation and differentiation from existing approaches.
- Identify major technical, legal, regulatory, or data-related barriers and potential mitigation measures.

### Market Size & Competitive Analysis

**Target Market and Customer Segmentation**

- Identify the primary target users, customer segments, and key beneficiaries.
- Explain their needs, adoption motivations, and expected value from the solution.

**Market Sizing and Demand**

- Assess the market opportunity and potential demand for the proposed solution, using relevant market data, research, or evidence where available.
- Support market demand assumptions with credible data, research, or evidence.

**Revenue Model**

- Outline the proposed revenue streams, cost structure, and financial sustainability.

**Competitive Landscape**

- Identify key competitors and existing alternatives.
- Analyze relevant competitors and existing alternatives, highlighting their strengths, limitations, and how the proposed solution differentiates itself.
- Explain the Unique Selling Proposition (USP), why users would adopt the solution over existing alternatives, and the competitive barriers that could protect it from rapid imitation.

### Strategic Contribution & Expected Impact

- Explain how the solution addresses the root problem and improves the effectiveness, reliability, accessibility, or sustainability of Vietnam's financial system.
- Identify the intended beneficiaries and the scale of potential socio-economic impact.
- Define measurable indicators or methods for evaluating the solution's outcomes.
- Explain how the solution can sustain long-term value creation without relying entirely on external funding or subsidies.
- Address relevant risks, adaptability to changing conditions, and the balance of benefits among stakeholders.

**SDG Alignment:** Explain how the solution contributes to the relevant United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly:

- SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth
- SDG 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
- SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production
- SDG 13: Climate Action
- SDG 16: Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

Teams should justify the relevance of the selected SDGs rather than merely listing them.

### Implementation Roadmap & Scalability

**Implementation Roadmap**

- Present a visual quarterly milestone plan from the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) through testing, validation, and ecosystem partner integration.
- Identify key implementation stages, milestones, and dependencies.

**Scalability and Infrastructure**

- Explain how the solution can expand to additional users, customer segments, financial products, or markets without requiring proportional increases in operating costs.
- Describe how the core architecture supports integration with banks, fintechs, enterprises, and other ecosystem partners.
- Explain infrastructure requirements, operational cost efficiency, and the potential to expand into other regions or markets.

### References

List all sources, datasets, and frameworks cited in the proposal. Prioritize credible, relevant sources that reflect Vietnam's financial system and market conditions. Use a consistent referencing style.

### Appendix (Optional)

Include supplementary materials such as extended figures, supporting datasets, technical notes, assumptions, or detailed calculations. Appendices are supplementary and will not be scored; essential information must remain in the main report.
